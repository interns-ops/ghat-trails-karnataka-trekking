FAQ · Updated 20 November 2026

Karnataka trekking questions

Short answers to what trekkers ask most. Each links to the full guide.

Which is the highest peak in Karnataka?

Mullayanagiri in Chikkamagaluru district, at about 1,930 m above sea level. Read the Mullayanagiri trek guide.

What is the best beginner trek near Bengaluru?

Skandagiri is the usual first trek. It is roughly 60 to 70 km north of the city, climbs to about 1,350 m and takes three to four hours round trip. See the Skandagiri trek guide.

What is the best time to trek in Karnataka?

October to February. Skies are clearer, grass is green after the rains and most forest trails are open. The season guide goes month by month.

Do I need a permit to trek in Karnataka?

Many popular routes, including Kudremukh and Kumara Parvata, cross forest land and need advance permission from the Karnataka Forest Department. Rules change, so confirm on the official Karnataka Forest Department booking portal before you travel.

Can I trek in Karnataka during the monsoon?

Not on most Western Ghats routes. Heavy rain, leeches, slippery rock and temporary forest closures make June to September risky. Short, low-altitude walks are the safer choice.

What should I carry on a day trek?

Two to three litres of water, trail snacks, a rain shell, shoes with grip, a head torch, a basic first-aid kit and a copy of your permit. The gear checklist has the detail.

Which is the hardest of these Karnataka treks?

Kumara Parvata. It is about 26 km round trip, usually over two days, with a steep forest climb and rock sections. See the Kumara Parvata trek guide.

Is it safe to trek alone in Karnataka?

Forest routes can require a guide or a group under permit rules, and mobile signal is patchy on the ridges. Go with others, tell someone your plan and carry a copy of your permit.
