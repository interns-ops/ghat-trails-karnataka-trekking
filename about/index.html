About Ghat Trails

Ghat Trails is a short, practical guide to five treks in the Western Ghats of Karnataka.

How the guides are made

Each trek page gives one clear answer first, then the route, the practical details and the best months. Peak heights are commonly published figures and may differ by a few metres between sources. Distances and times are approximate round-trip figures for an average walker.

Sources

The guides rely on published peak heights and on guidance from the Karnataka Forest Department about permits and entry. Peak heights can be checked against Survey of India publications. Rules, quotas and trail conditions change often, so treat these pages as a starting point and confirm with the local forest office before you travel.

Corrections

If a height, distance or rule looks wrong, tell the site owner and the page will be updated and re-dated. Start from the trek comparison or the FAQ.
