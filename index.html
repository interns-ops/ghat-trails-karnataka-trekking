Best treks in Karnataka: 5 peaks compared

Five peaks, from a four-hour fort climb to a two-day forest ridge, with the height, distance, difficulty and months that matter for each.

Quick answer

Start with Skandagiri or Mullayanagiri if you are new to trekking. Choose Tadiandamol or Kudremukh for a full day on the trail, and keep Kumara Parvata for when you are fit and have a permit. Go between October and February.

Summit height above sea level. Difficulty depends more on distance, slope and weather than on this number, which is why the 1,712 m peak is the hardest of the five.

Plan before you go

The Western Ghats get most of their rain from June to September. Trails are open, dry and clear from the first cool weeks of October, so most of these treks are best between October and February. The best time to trek in Karnataka guide breaks this down month by month.

Pack for sun, wind and sudden rain, even on a short trek. The trekking gear checklist lists what to carry and why.

Forest permits are needed on several routes and the rules change. Read the permit question in the FAQ, then confirm on the Karnataka Forest Department's Aranya Vihaara booking portal.

Quick permit answers

Do I need a permit to trek in Karnataka?

Many popular routes, including Kudremukh and Kumara Parvata, cross forest land and need advance permission from the Karnataka Forest Department. Rules change, so confirm on the official booking portal before you travel.

Can I trek in Karnataka during the monsoon?

Not on most Western Ghats routes. Heavy rain, leeches, slippery rock and temporary forest closures make June to September risky. Short, low-altitude walks are the safer choice.
