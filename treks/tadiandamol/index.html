Tadiandamol trek

Tadiandamol is the highest peak in Kodagu at about 1,748 m. The day trek from Kakkabe covers roughly 12 km round trip and takes five to six hours.

What the Tadiandamol trek is like

The route starts near Kakkabe village and climbs through coffee and forest patches onto open grass ridges, with shola forest in the folds of the hills. The final stretch is exposed, and the wind at the top can be strong enough to need a jacket.

Planning your day

Start early so you finish before afternoon cloud and rain. Carry at least two litres of water, because there is little reliable water on the ridge, and a rain shell in any month, since showers arrive quickly in Kodagu.

Best time to go

October to March is the main window, and the hills are greenest just after the monsoon. Avoid June to September, when rain, mist and leeches make the trail hard going and local access can be restricted. The season guide explains why.

Questions about the Tadiandamol trek

How hard is the Tadiandamol trek?

It is moderate. The distance and the climb make it a full day, but there are no technical sections.

Where does the Tadiandamol trek start?

Most trekkers start from Kakkabe village in Kodagu.
