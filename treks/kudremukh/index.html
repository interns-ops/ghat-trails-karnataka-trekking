Kudremukh trek

Kudremukh, meaning horse face in Kannada, rises to about 1,894 m inside Kudremukh National Park. The route is roughly 20 km round trip and takes seven to nine hours.

What the Kudremukh trek is like

The trail crosses rolling grass hills with long stretches of open ground and very little shade. Sholas fill the folds between the ridges, and the slanted profile of the peak gives the mountain its name. The distance, rather than the steepness, is what makes it a full day.

Permits and planning

The trek lies inside a national park, so you need permission from the Karnataka Forest Department, and entry rules and quotas can change. Book through the official forest department channel in advance and carry a copy. Kalasa is the usual base.

Start at first light and carry plenty of water and sun protection.

Best time to go

October to February is the most reliable window. In the monsoon the grass slopes are slippery and the park may restrict entry. See the season guide.

Questions about the Kudremukh trek

Do I need a permit for Kudremukh?

Usually, yes. The trek is inside Kudremukh National Park, so permission from the Karnataka Forest Department is normally required. Confirm the current process before you travel.

How long is the Kudremukh trek?

About 20 km round trip, which most people finish in seven to nine hours.
