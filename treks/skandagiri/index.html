Skandagiri trek

Skandagiri, also called Kalavara Durga, rises to about 1,350 m north of Bengaluru. The rocky climb to the ruined fort takes three to four hours round trip and is the usual first trek from the city.

What the Skandagiri trek is like

The trail climbs over rocky ground and stone steps to the ruined walls of an old hill fort. It is short, but the final stretch is steep and exposed, so take it slowly.

On many winter mornings, cloud fills the valleys below the fort, which is why the climb is popular at first light.

Entry rules and crowds

Skandagiri is close to Bengaluru, so weekends are very busy. Access times, and sunrise or night entry, have been restricted in the past, so confirm the current rules with the Karnataka Forest Department or the local office before you travel. Weekday visits are quieter.

Best time to go

October to February is cooler and clearer. Summer mornings get hot quickly and the bare rock holds heat, and monsoon rain makes the rock slippery. See the season guide for month-by-month advice.

Questions about the Skandagiri trek

How far is Skandagiri from Bengaluru?

It is roughly 60 to 70 km north of the city, near Chikkaballapur.

Is Skandagiri good for beginners?

Yes, it is a short trek, though the last section is steep and rocky, so wear shoes with good grip.
