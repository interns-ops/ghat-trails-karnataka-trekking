Kumara Parvata trek

Kumara Parvata, on the Pushpagiri ridge near Kukke Subramanya, rises to about 1,712 m. The trek is roughly 26 km round trip, usually over two days, and is the hardest of the five.

What the Kumara Parvata trek is like

The route begins near Kukke Subramanya and climbs steeply through forest before opening onto rock and grass towards the ridge. The climb is long and relentless, and the steep rock sections need steady footing, good fitness and a head for exposure.

Permits and planning

The route passes through protected forest, so an advance permit from the Karnataka Forest Department is normally required, and it often comes with conditions on group size, guides and camping.

Carry food and water for the full trip, a head torch, warm layers for the night and a printed copy of your permit.

Best time to go

November to February is the usual window, after the forest has dried out. Avoid the monsoon completely. See the season guide.

Questions about the Kumara Parvata trek

Why is Kumara Parvata considered hard?

The distance, the steep forest climb and the rock sections add up over two days, and weather can change quickly on the ridge.

Can I do Kumara Parvata in one day?

Most people plan two days. A single day is very demanding, and permit conditions may also decide the plan.
