Trekking gear checklist for Karnataka trails

For a day trek in Karnataka, carry two to three litres of water, shoes with good grip, a rain shell, a head torch and a copy of your permit.

Day trek essentials

  • Water, 2–3 litresStreams on grass ridges dry up by December.
  • Shoes with gripWet laterite and granite slabs are slick.
  • Rain shellShowers arrive without warning, even in winter.
  • Head torchSunrise treks start in the dark.
  • Permit copyForest check posts ask for a printed or saved booking.
  • Salt and sugarElectrolyte sachets and glucose for long, exposed climbs.

Extra for a two-day trek

The two-day route to Kumara Parvata needs everything above plus these.

  • Warm layerNights on the ridge are cold even in the cool season.
  • Food for the full tripThere are no shops on the route.
  • Extra battery or power bankMobile signal is patchy, so save battery.
  • Basic first-aid kitBlister plasters, antiseptic, pain relief and any personal medicines.

Leave the trail clean

Carry out all wrapping and waste, keep to the marked path on grass slopes and do not light fires inside forest areas. Ready to choose a route? Compare them in the trek comparison table, or check the best months to go.
