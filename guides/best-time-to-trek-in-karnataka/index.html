Best time to trek in Karnataka

October to February is the best time to trek in Karnataka. The air is cooler, the skies are clearer and most forest trails are open.

What each season is like

October to February: post-monsoon and winter

Skies are clear, mornings are cool and the hills are green in October and November. December and January are the coldest and driest months, with the best visibility. Most forest trails and permits are open.

March to May: summer

Afternoons are hot, views turn hazy and the grass dries out. Plan exposed routes such as Kudremukh for first light only, and carry extra water.

June to September: monsoon

Heavy rain, slippery rock, leeches and thick cloud make most Western Ghats treks risky, and many forest trails close or restrict entry. Short, low-altitude walks are the safer choice, and you should check with forest offices before you plan anything.
